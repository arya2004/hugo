# Reach

Hugo targets repository maintainers, contributors and funders who want to inspect contribution eligibility and payment references independently of the private reward database.

Try the deployed feature at https://hugo-three-amber.vercel.app/arkiv and its live query at https://hugo-three-amber.vercel.app/api/arkiv/contributions. One demonstration contribution is visible, marked `ELIGIBLE` and `RESERVED;paid=1/1` when checked on 6 November 2026.

This demonstrates the integration, not an active-user count or adoption claim. Post-event maintenance and rollout commitments have not been confirmed by the maintainer.
