# Code of Conduct

We want Hugo to be a welcoming place to collaborate. Be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and make space for people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and code without attacking people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing someone else's private information are unacceptable. Respect requests to stop unwanted interactions.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. To report a concern privately, contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Include relevant links and context; maintainers should handle reports discreetly and respond fairly.
